Privacy Policy

Complete Waste of Time (CWOT) · Alexander Lanser · Effective October 29, 2026
The short version: CWOT does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything the game remembers about you stays on your device.

1. Who I am

Complete Waste of Time ("CWOT", "the game", "the app") is published by Alexander Lanser, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game does and does not handle.

2. Information I collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not collect device identifiers for tracking.

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a game, CWOT saves your progress locally on your iPhone: coin balance, unlocked characters, worlds and pipes, high scores and the name you type beside them, and your settings (difficulty, sounds, music, language). This data never leaves your device, is not sent to me, and is deleted when you delete the app. You can clear your high scores and settings yourself at any time using the reset buttons in the game's Settings screen.

4. In-app purchases

CWOT offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs and the Coin Doubler). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never see or store your payment details. Apple shares with the game only the information needed to deliver what you bought (for example, that a purchase was completed or restored). Apple's handling of your payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Ratings and reviews

After you have played for a while, the game may invite you to rate it. This prompt is shown by Apple's built-in review system; I do not receive any information about whether you saw it, dismissed it, or what you chose.

6. Analytics, advertising and third parties

CWOT contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs). I do not sell, rent, or share any information with anyone, because I have none to share.

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal information and are used only to fix bugs.

7. Children's privacy

CWOT is rated 9+ and is suitable for a general audience. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. I comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by collecting no data at all.

8. Your rights

Because I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, export or delete. Everything the game stores lives on your device under your control. If you have a question about this, you can still contact me at the address below and I will respond.

9. Security

Your game data is stored only on your own device and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption). Purchase transactions are secured by Apple.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if I add an online feature), I will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy: cwot.app@gmail.com
