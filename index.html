Frequently asked questions

I bought the Coin Doubler on another iPhone. How do I get it here?
Open the Coin Store and tap Restore Purchases. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID that bought it. Coin packs are consumable and are not restorable — they were delivered to the balance on the device that bought them.
What does the Coin Doubler actually do?
Every coin you collect in gameplay is worth double, forever. The same number of coins fly to your counter; each one lands worth +2 instead of +1. It does not double coins bought in a coin pack.
I reset my progress by mistake. Can you restore my coins?
Unfortunately no. Your progress is stored only on your own iPhone and never sent to me, so there is nothing on my side to restore. The Coin Doubler survives a reset and a reinstall because it's tied to your Apple ID.
How do I get a refund for a purchase?
All purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
Does the game collect any of my data?
No. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
Can I change the difficulty or turn off the music?
Yes — both are in Settings, along with the language (English / Español), the tutorial, and each sound effect.
